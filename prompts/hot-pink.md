# Prompts: Hot pink redesign + login illustration

## Prompt
> Can you change the colors of the app, maybe add some pictures, and make it look more catchy?

The agent asked two questions first. I picked the **hot pink pop** palette (out of hot pink, editorial black & red, or Indo-western jewel tones) and said to try **an illustration on the login page first** and see how it looks.

## What the agent built
- New palette everywhere: blush background (#FFF1F5), hot pink (#E8337A) for buttons, stars, and the chart, deep plum (#3B0A2A) for text, and a little tangerine (#FF8A3D) in the illustration.
- New fonts: Fraunces (a chunky, soft serif) for headings and the logo, DM Sans for everything else.
- Login and sign-up are now split screen: a hot pink panel with a big "Closet Log" wordmark and a hand-drawn clothes rail (slip dress, striped tee, trousers, cardigan, heels, sparkles) next to the form. On phones the illustration sits on top.
- The clothes swing on the rail once when the page loads, then settle. Turned off for people who've asked their device to reduce motion.
- The illustration is drawn in code (SVG), so there are no image files to load.
